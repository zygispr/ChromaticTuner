Privacy Policy

%CT_APP_NAME% collects nothing. This page says what that means in practice, because a policy that only says "we respect your privacy" gives you nothing you can check.

What the app does with sound

%CT_PURPOSE_SENTENCE%

The microphone is open only while the tuner is listening. Leaving or closing the app releases it, and the recording indicator your device shows goes out at the same moment.

What is collected

Nothing at all — there is no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising identifier.

  • No audio leaves your device. Sound is measured as it arrives and then discarded; nothing is written to storage and nothing is uploaded.
  • Nothing is kept about what you played, when you played it, or for how long.
  • There is nothing to create and nothing to join, so there is no name, address, or identifier of yours for the app to hold.

The one permission the app asks for

The microphone, and nothing else. A tuner either hears the string or guesses at it, so this permission is what the app is for rather than an extra it would be convenient to have. It is requested when you first start tuning, and you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings — the app then stops hearing anything and shows you why.

Where the audio goes

Nowhere. The app makes no network requests while you use it, which is a property of how it is built rather than a promise about how it is operated: an automated check in this project fails the build if any request is made during use. After the first load it works with no network connection at all.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children either.

If this ever changes

If the app ever begins doing something this page does not describe, this page changes in the same release that changes the app — it is stored beside the source and reviewed with it, not published separately afterwards. The date below is when it last changed.
